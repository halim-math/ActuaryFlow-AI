# Demo Motor Policy

> Synthetic policy wording for software tests only. It is not a real insurance contract.

## Covered loss
Subject to the declarations, exclusions, deductible, and limits, the demo contract covers direct accidental physical damage to the insured vehicle caused by collision, fire, theft, or vandalism during the policy period.

## Deductible
For a covered physical damage event, the insured retains the first EUR 500 of loss unless a different deductible is stated in the declarations.

## Exclusions
The demo contract excludes intentional loss by the insured, use in organized racing, loss arising while the vehicle is used for undisclosed commercial passenger transport, and ordinary wear and tear.

## Duties after loss
The insured should protect the property from further damage, notify the insurer promptly, provide reasonable documentation, and cooperate with investigation.

## Decision authority
Coverage decisions require review of the complete contract and facts. Automated retrieval or calculation output is decision support only.
