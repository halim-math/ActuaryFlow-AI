# Demo Home Policy

> Synthetic wording for retrieval and evaluation. Not a real policy.

## Property coverage
The demo contract describes coverage for sudden and accidental direct physical loss to the listed dwelling from named causes including fire, lightning, windstorm, and certain escape-of-water events, subject to limits and exclusions.

## Water limitation
Continuous seepage, long-term leakage, groundwater, flood, storm surge, and failure to take reasonable mitigation steps are outside this synthetic example unless specifically endorsed.

## Additional living expense
When a covered event makes the residence uninhabitable, reasonable additional living expense may be considered up to the stated sublimit and time period.

## Human review
Ambiguous causation, policy conflicts, suspected fraud, material coverage disputes, and high-severity losses must be escalated to an authorized reviewer.
